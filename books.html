The Series
The Adventures of Raspen & Sylas

A heartwarming children's series following two unlikely friends — a rabbit and a squirrel — through the kind of life lessons Sara and her brother grew up learning side by side: friendship, kindness, courage, and doing what's right. Written for ages 2–6.

Book 1 · The Beginning
A Tale of Friendship

How it all began. Raspen the rabbit and Sylas the squirrel — two unlikely friends — discover that the best adventures start with an open heart. A gentle introduction to kindness, sharing, and the quiet courage it takes to make a new friend.

Lesson Friendship
Ages 2–6
Available on Amazon — Free on Kindle Unlimited
Book 2 · The Adventure Continues
The Bear That Won't Share

Deeper into the forest, Raspen and Sylas meet Brenor, a bear who keeps everything for himself. Through patience and a little bravery, they learn that sharing isn't losing — it's how friendships grow. A story about generosity, empathy, and standing up for what's fair.

Lesson Sharing
Ages 2–6
Available on Amazon — Free on Kindle Unlimited
Book 3 · A Sunlit Coast
Rune the Sneaky Raccoon

Raspen and Sylas venture beyond the redwoods to a sunlit coast, where they meet Rune — a clever raccoon whose tricks test the very things that make their friendship strong. When sneaky choices cause trouble, Rune discovers that telling the truth is the bravest choice of all.

Lesson Honesty & Trust
Ages 2–6
Available on Amazon
Book 4 · A Brave Step
Koa Discovers Courage

Raspen and Sylas meet Koa, a little turtle facing something that scares him. With his friends cheering him on, Koa finds out that courage doesn't mean not being scared. It means trying anyway.

Lesson Courage
Ages 2–6
Available on Amazon
Book 5 · Finding Your Voice
Pax Finds His Voice

On a forest trail, Raspen and Sylas help Pax, a gentle porcupine pulling a wagon piled high for his family picnic. Along the way, the quiet porcupine discovers that his ideas matter and his voice deserves to be heard.

Lesson Speaking Up
Ages 2–6
Available on Amazon
Book 6 · Newest Adventure
Bodhi's Not So Perfect Plan

Near a sparkling creek, Raspen and Sylas meet Bodhi, a busy beaver who wants his dam to look exactly like his drawing. When the branches won't cooperate, the friends find out that plans can change, and not everything has to be perfect to be wonderful.

Lesson Flexibility & Teamwork
Ages 2–6
